Know the Risks

Disaster Information

Learn basic safety actions for different disaster situations.

01

🌊 Flood

Move to higher ground, avoid flood water and follow official evacuation instructions.

02

🌎 Earthquake

Drop, cover and hold on. Stay away from windows and unstable structures.

03

🔥 Fire

Leave the building immediately and avoid using elevators during a fire emergency.

04

🌪 Cyclone

Stay indoors, secure loose objects and follow official weather warnings.

05

⚡ Lightning

Move indoors and avoid open spaces, isolated trees and metal structures.

06

🏭 Industrial Accident

Move away from hazardous areas and follow the instructions of emergency authorities.
